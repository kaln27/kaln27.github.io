Hi! I'm **Juncheng Liao (廖俊程)**, a master's student in Software Engineering at Zhejiang University. I previously studied Intelligent Vehicle Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology.

My interests sit at the intersection of multimodal large language models, efficient model training and inference, and continual learning. I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable systems and contributing the resulting improvements back to open-source projects.
