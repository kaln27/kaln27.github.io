AWARe paper figures
===================

These figures are exported from the original figure PDFs used in the AWARe
manuscript (`main.pdf`). They retain the paper's original artwork and data.
The PNGs have a maximum dimension of 1600 pixels and are displayed with a
click-to-enlarge control on the introduction page.

| Website asset | Paper figure | Original figure asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `method-detail.png` | Figure 3, p. 4 | `Method.pdf` |
| `q-proj-retention.png` | Figure 4(a), p. 6 | `q_proj_heatmap.pdf` |
| `k-proj-retention.png` | Figure 4(b), p. 6 | `k_proj_heatmap.pdf` |
| `v-proj-retention.png` | Figure 4(c), p. 6 | `v_proj_heatmap.pdf` |
| `results-overview.png` | Figure 1, p. 1 | `mllm_dcl_combined.pdf` |
| `training-target-ablation.png` | Figure 5, p. 8 | `training_target_ablation.pdf` |

The results dialogs also show high-resolution crops of Table 1 (p. 6) and
Table 2 (p. 7) from `files/aware-paper.pdf`. They preserve the original rows,
columns, and highlights; the dialogs link to the paper for the captions.
