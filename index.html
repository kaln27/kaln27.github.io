Juncheng Liao 廖俊程

I am a master's student in Software Engineering at Zhejiang University. I received my B.Eng. in Intelligent Vehicle Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2025.

My research interests lie in multimodal large language models, continual learning, and efficient training and inference. I am interested in how models can adapt to new tasks while retaining what they have already learned.

I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable systems, and contributing the resulting improvements back to open-source projects.
