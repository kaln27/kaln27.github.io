Research / Engineering

Juncheng Liao 廖俊程

I am a master's student in Software Engineering at Zhejiang University. I received my B.Eng. in Intelligent Vehicle Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2025.

My research interests lie in multimodal large language models, continual learning, and efficient training and inference. I am interested in how models can adapt to new tasks while retaining what they have already learned.

I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable systems, and contributing the resulting improvements back to open-source projects.

Omni-modal LLMs · Efficient inference

OmniKey: Lifecycle-Aware Audiovisual Token Compression for Omni-Modal Large Language Models

Juncheng Liao · Second author

A training-free framework combines audiovisual deduplication, Key-alignment-guided progressive pruning, and late-layer token exit to reduce computation and KV-cache overhead. Compatible with FlashAttention.

On Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, retaining 25% of audiovisual tokens preserves 98.6% of average performance across four benchmarks, with 3.44× decoder prefill speedup on WorldSense and 4.55 GiB lower peak memory than the full-token input.
